Security Frameworks helps your organization work through the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0, NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, and NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3, one requirement at a time.

Each requirement becomes a plain-language question, with why it matters, what done looks like, and practical next steps. NIST's own text is always one tap away.

WORK THROUGH YOUR FRAMEWORKS
• Keep separate assessments for each organization or review period
• Turn on the frameworks you need and choose an SP 800-53 baseline: Low, Moderate, or High, with Privacy optional
• Answer requirements in a guided questionnaire, or browse by function, family, and category
• Record status, owners, target dates, notes, and organization-defined values
• Attach evidence: notes, links, photos, and files

SEE THE BIG PICTURE
• Follow a prioritized list of next steps across every framework you use
• Track progress on dashboards for each framework, function, and baseline
• Build a CSF 2.0 target profile and compare current and target tiers
• See where requirements line up across frameworks using NIST-published mappings, and apply one answer to related requirements

SHARE AND REPORT
• Export a PDF report, a spreadsheet, or a data file, and choose what to include, such as leaving out internal notes
• Send an encrypted copy of an assessment to teammates or a new device. When a teammate sends their copy back, their updates merge with yours

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your device. No account, no servers, no analytics, no tracking
• Optional Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode lock
• Shared files are encrypted with AES-256 using a password you choose

Built for iPhone and iPad, with multitasking, keyboard shortcuts, pointer support, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver.

IMPORTANT
Security Frameworks is a self-assessment aid. It is not a certification, an audit, or a compliance attestation. It is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). Framework text comes from NIST publications. The plain-language guidance was written for this app; it summarizes NIST's text but does not replace it.
